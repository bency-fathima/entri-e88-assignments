About Us

Soofi Mandi started as a small family kitchen bringing authentic Arabic Mandhi to Kerala. Every dish is slow-cooked the traditional way, with fragrant basmati rice and secret spice blends passed down through generations, served the way it was meant to be shared — with family and friends.
