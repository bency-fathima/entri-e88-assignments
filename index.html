About Us

ZAYKA began with a simple idea: to bring people together through the comfort of delicious food. Inspired by the rich culinary traditions of India, our restaurant combines familiar flavors with a modern dining experience. We believe every meal should be more than just food—it should be a moment worth sharing. From our kitchen to your table, we prepare every dish with fresh ingredients, traditional spices, and a lot of care.
