About us

Welcome to The Grand Hotel, where culinary traditions meet exceptional taste. We take pride in offering a diverse menu that brings together the authentic flavors of Kerala, rich Middle Eastern delicacies, and classic North Indian favorites. From our signature Kerala Fish Curry and tender Mutton Mandi to comforting sweet treats like Kunafa and Elaneer Pudding, every dish is crafted with passion and the finest ingredients. Our welcoming ambiance and flavorful creations promise an unforgettable dining experience. Come, taste the goodness, and leave with happiness.
